Questions? We’ve got you

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch your order within 1–2 working days, and it usually reaches you 3–5 working days after that. You get tracking details by SMS or email as soon as it ships.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes, cash on delivery is available across India. You can also pay online by UPI, debit or credit card, net banking or wallets.

How do I choose my size?

Our sizes run from 36 to 50, and each product page shows the sizes in stock. Check our size guide, or WhatsApp us your bust measurement and we’ll suggest the right size.

What if the size doesn’t fit?

No worries, we’ll happily help you exchange it for another size or another piece you’ll love. Just WhatsApp us with your order number within 12 hours of delivery. Since every piece is handcrafted, we don’t offer returns or refunds, so feel free to message us before ordering and we’ll help you pick the perfect size. Exchange policy

How do I wash chikankari?

Hand wash gently in cold water with a mild liquid wash, dry in the shade and iron inside out. Dry clean dark shades. Here’s our full chikankari care guide.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, we ship worldwide. See international orders for details.

Is the embroidery really done by hand?

Yes. Every piece is hand-embroidered by artisans in Lucknow, so small variations in the stitches are part of what makes it yours.
