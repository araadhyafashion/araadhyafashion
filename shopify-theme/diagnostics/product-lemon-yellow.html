Questions, answered

Is the embroidery done by hand?

Yes. Every piece is hand-embroidered by artisans in Lucknow, so tiny variations are a sign of real handwork.

Will the colour fade or bleed after washing?

Hand wash separately in cold water for the first wash, dry in shade and dry clean dark shades. With this care the colour stays rich.

How do I pick the right size?

Use the size chart above, or our full size guide. The size number is your bust in inches. Between two sizes? Pick the larger one, or WhatsApp us and we'll help.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes, COD is available across India. We confirm COD orders on WhatsApp before dispatch.

When will my order arrive?

Orders are dispatched in 1–2 working days and usually delivered 3–5 working days after dispatch. We also ship worldwide (4–7 working days after dispatch).

Will the fabric shrink?

Slight shrinkage (up to 5%) is natural in handcrafted fabrics. With proper care it stays around 2%.
